# 01 — Glossary

## Current
- **Maieutics** [G] : the art of bringing ideas to birth. The skill, and this project.
- **Corpus** [G] : the project's written memory — notes, glossary, intent, logs. Lives in `corpus/`.
- ***Partus*** [C] : Latin for *a birth, a delivery, that which is brought forth*. The deliverables directory. From *parere*, to bring forth — the same root as *parent*. *Alternative considered: `edita`, from* edere, *to bring forth and also to publish.*

- ***Instrumenta*** [G] : Latin for *the instruments*. In Latin medical usage, the midwife's own instruments. The scripts directory.
- ***Limbus*** [G] : Latin for *the hem, the border*; in theology, limbo — where the unbaptised infants go. Abandoned ideas: what was not brought to term.
- ***Vestigia*** [G] : Latin for *the traces, the footprints*. Superseded versions, the path already walked.
- ***Abortus*** [C] : *that which was not carried to term*. Thematically the exact word for abandoned pistes — **deliberately not used**: too loaded on a public repository to carry anything else.
